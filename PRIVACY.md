# Privacy policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

Out of Frame doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **Your settings stay on your device.** The list of sites, the stream mode switch and the optional text are saved in Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They aren't synced or sent anywhere.
- **Pages are compared, never collected.** To decide whether to cover a page, the extension compares its address with your list, inside your browser. While a page is covered, its tab title is replaced with a masked one, then restored. Nothing about the page is stored or sent.
- **No network requests.** No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code.

The source code is public: https://github.com/flav-code/out-of-frame. Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/flav-code/out-of-frame/issues).
